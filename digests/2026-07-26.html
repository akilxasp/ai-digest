The New Skill Is Routing, Not Prompting
Three unrelated stories today rhyme. Echo strings together cheap open-weight models to match a frontier model's output at a third of the cost. Anthropic just turned its priciest model into a rationed perk instead of a default. And 25 companies are publicly lobbying to keep open-weight models legal to use at all. The frontier model is becoming one option in a portfolio, not the whole answer — knowing when to reach for it, versus a cheaper specialist, is turning into the actual differentiator. The tools built today follow the same instinct: Bento and Palmier Pro don't replace Claude Code, they wrap around it.

Daily Tip
Before defaulting to your most expensive model for every task, try running a cheap open-weight model first and only escalating the parts it gets wrong — Echo's whole premise is that most of a task doesn't need the frontier model, just the hard slice does.
