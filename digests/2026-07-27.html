Daily Tip
When a new flagship model drops, don't just swap it in — re-run your existing evals against it first. Today's Opus 5 launch is a good example: a cheaper model that beats its predecessor on some benchmarks can still regress on your specific task, so a quick side-by-side on your real prompts beats trusting the leaderboard.
