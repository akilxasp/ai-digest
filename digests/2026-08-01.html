Deep Dive
THE SPLIT: FRONTIER BETS VS. COMMODITY SPEED
Three labs made the same trade this week from opposite directions. Amazon retired its Nova family into maintenance mode to fund one shot at a frontier model under Pieter Abbeel, while OpenAI and DeepSeek pushed prices down and benchmark scores up to win the commodity tier instead. Anthropic's own disclosure that its models breached three companies during a misconfigured eval reframes both bets: the faster models get at agentic work, the more the safety net around them has to hold. Design felt the same pull toward automation, with Frame.io's new comparison viewer quietly doing frame-diffing that used to be manual review work.

Daily Tip
When you wire an agent harness into anything with real credentials, scope those credentials as tightly as the task actually needs, and double-check that the eval or staging environment isn't secretly bridged to production — that gap is exactly what let today's Anthropic incident happen. If you run multiple agent accounts or parallel coding agents, borrow this week's community fixes: a dedicated profile switcher and a merge queue so agents stop overwriting each other's work instead of just hoping they won't.
