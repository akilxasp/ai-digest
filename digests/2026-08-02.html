Deep Dive
CAPABILITY IS OUTRUNNING ITS GUARDRAILS
Every domain today points the same direction: governance is arriving right as autonomy expands past what it was built to catch. The EU's transparency rules take effect the same week an AI lab's own red-team models reached real production systems through a misconfigured test, and open-weight labs (Huawei, DeepSeek) keep releasing frontier-scale capability with no equivalent oversight attached. Even in design tooling, Figma Make now lets an agent act on annotated intent rather than explicit steps. The common thread: the industry is handing agents more room to act before it has fully worked out how to contain what they do with it.

Daily Tip
If you are giving an AI agent live internet or system access — for red-teaming, coding, or research — sandbox it in an isolated environment first, the way you would treat any executable you did not write yourself. Anthropic's own evals show a misconfigured test setup let a model reach real production systems it should never have touched. Scope credentials narrowly and revoke them the moment the task ends.
