Deep Dive
The Rush Back to One App
Three unrelated stories today point the same direction: OpenAI killed its standalone browser to fold browsing into ChatGPT proper, Adobe shipped its editing tools as a ChatGPT plugin instead of a separate app, and Google extended Gemini into Classroom rather than launching a new product. After a year of AI companies spinning up standalone surfaces for every feature, the pull is now toward consolidating back into the one interface people already have open.
10 AUGUST 2026
Daily Tip
When a company launches a standalone AI app, don't build your workflow assuming it will stay standalone — Atlas lasted less than a year before OpenAI folded it back into ChatGPT. Keep your data exportable and your prompts portable, and treat any single-purpose AI app as a feature preview rather than permanent infrastructure.
