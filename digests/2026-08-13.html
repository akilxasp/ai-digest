But the loudest praise this week ran the other way — Ante and Needle2, two Show HN agents that fit in double-digit megabytes and run fully offline. Daily Tip
When a task is scoped and repetitive, don't reach for the frontier model by default — the offline agents blowing up on Hacker News this week (Ante, Needle2) handle that class of work at a fraction of the latency and cost. Save the big context-window models for genuinely open-ended problems.
