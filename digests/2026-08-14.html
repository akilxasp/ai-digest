Deep Dive
The Real Race Is Now Infrastructure, Not Models
Three separate stories today point at the same thing: Anthropic buying a chip-efficiency startup, Cerebras partnering with OpenAI on faster inference, and Google shipping a new Flash model three weeks after the last one — all bets on squeezing more out of existing compute rather than just training bigger. The same shift shows up downstream: Figma's growth is coming from AI credit consumption, not new seats, and community energy has moved to agent harnesses and memory systems — DeepSeek's new Harness, MCP Memory, context compaction in Pi — rather than new chat models. The tooling layer underneath is where the real competition now lives.

Daily Tip
When a faster or cheaper model ships, like today's Gemini 3.7 Flash or Cerebras-accelerated GPT-5.6, don't swap it in blind. Rerun your existing eval set first — speed and price gains often trade off against instruction-following on the edge cases only your own prompts will catch.
