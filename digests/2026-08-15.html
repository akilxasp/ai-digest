Deep Dive
Autonomy Becomes a Dial, Not a Switch
Every domain shipped the same idea this week under different names: don't just turn agents loose, give people a knob for how loose. Claude Code's new default runs unattended but still stops at irreversible actions, DeepSeek's V4-Pro exposes a low/high/max reasoning-effort setting, and Framer lets designers trade thinking time for speed per generation. The competitive question in AI tools and design tools alike is quietly shifting from raw capability to how finely you can tune how much of it gets used.
15 AUGUST 2026
Daily Tip
When your agent harness exposes a reasoning-effort or thinking-budget knob, don't leave it maxed out by default. Dial it down for rote edits and formatting, and save high effort for genuinely ambiguous problems — you'll get faster iteration on the easy 80% of work without paying a quality tax on it.
