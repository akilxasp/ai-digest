Deep Dive
THE PROVENANCE LAYER ARRIVES
Two of today’s biggest AI stories are about proving where content came from — Anthropic’s SynthID-based text watermark and Google’s optional-removal image watermark both treat provenance as a shippable feature, not an afterthought. A smaller signal points the same way: Figma’s new text-wrap rules exist purely to make output more predictable and less fussy to check by hand. And the SpaceX–Cursor deal shows where the compute for all of this actually lives — increasingly inside companies that were never software companies to begin with.
16 AUGUST 2026
Daily Tip
If you’re pasting AI-drafted text into anything official, don’t assume it’s invisible — watermarking is becoming standard practice at both Anthropic and Google, and light edits won’t strip it. Treat AI output as a draft you visibly reshape, not a finished, unmarked deliverable; a full rewrite is the only way to remove attribution, and by then you’ve done most of the work yourself.
