Daily Tip
When a model starts overthinking a simple task — burning tokens on wildly long reasoning chains — cap it explicitly: set a low or medium reasoning effort, or a hard thinking-token budget, instead of just waiting it out. It's the same discipline behind picking infrastructure: bigger and pricier isn't automatically better, so match the model to the job first.
