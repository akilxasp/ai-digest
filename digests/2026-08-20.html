Deep Dive
VERIFICATION IS THE NEW MOAT
OpenAI's private, cross-session abuse screening, Z.ai's staged release of open weights, and D&AD's warning about vanishing junior creative roles all point the same direction: as models get more capable and more autonomous, trust is what buyers are shopping for, not raw capability. Figma's move to let anyone package and share an agent skill is the design-tool answer to the same problem — making AI behavior inspectable and repeatable instead of a black box.

Daily Tip
Before trusting a model's output on something that matters, run the same task past a second model or a held-out example, the way Netlify tested one prompt across 11 models this week. Cheap models often nail simple tasks and expensive ones don't always win; the only way to know is to check the specific task in front of you, not the leaderboard.
